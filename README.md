# A Genetic and Clustering algorithm-based tool to develop credit rating models for low default portfolios
This repository contains the results of my thesis project, completed as part of the requirements for the degree of Systems Engineering in Peru. The research focuses on the application of artificial intelligence techniques to develop credit risk classification models using genetic algorithms and hierarchical clustering.

## Abstract
This research proposes a method for generating a credit risk classification model based on the credit rating methodology. Implementing this methodology requires building two main analytical components: (1) a scoring model and (2) a rating‑group model. To build the credit rating model, this study employs two artificial intelligence techniques: (1) genetic algorithms to determine the optimal scoring model, and (2) agglomerative hierarchical clustering to define the risk‑group segmentation model. The experimental results show that the proposed approach achieves a strong statistical power ratio (58.2%). In addition, the model was compared with logistic regression —a well‑known statistical technique— and demonstrated a better performance. This study concludes that the artificial intelligence techniques used here perform effectively in generating a credit rating model, can operate with a low‑default portfolio, and produce results that are easily interpretable by human experts.
